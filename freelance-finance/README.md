## Setup Instructions
1. **Access the Template:** Open the source Google Sheet Template provided in the `templates/` folder (or via the secure link in your purchase email).
2. **Make a Copy:** Go to `File > Make a copy` to save your personal version.
3. **Configure the Setup Tab:** Enter your business information, current tax year, and customize your default categories.
4. **Start Tracking:** Log your income in the *Income Tracker* tab and record your expenses (with receipt links) in the *Expense Log*.
5. **View Your Dashboard:** Sit back and let the *Dashboard* tab automatically summarize your financial health.
